PhishGuard Lite is on

It checks the links in your email while you read, and puts a badge next to the risky ones.

What a warning looks like

  • Your parcel is waiting. Track your parcel

  • Action needed on your account. Confirm your account

  • Your invoice is ready. View invoice

  • Your statement is ready. Open PayPal

    No badge here: nothing suspicious was found in this link.

These are examples and the links don't open. Point at a badge, or tab to it, to see why the link was flagged and where it really goes.

Where it works

  • Gmail
  • Outlook on the web
  • LinkedIn

Outlook on the web includes outlook.office.com, outlook.live.com and outlook.cloud.microsoft. It doesn't run in the Outlook desktop or phone apps.

Check that it's working

While you're in Gmail, Outlook or LinkedIn, the PhishGuard icon in your toolbar shows what it found on that page:

✓No risky links on the page
2Two links were flagged (red when any is High Risk)

Can't see the icon? Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin PhishGuard Lite.

Your privacy

Links are checked inside your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you click Report on a flagged link, and then only the link, its text, why it was flagged and the site name.
