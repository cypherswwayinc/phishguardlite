What you see is not always where you go

Phishing emails rely on one trick: the text of a link can say anything. PhishGuard Lite ignores the text and checks the real address. These are the kinds of links it flags.

  1. Add it to Chrome

    Risky links get a Caution or High Risk badge. Hover over the badge to see why, and where the link really goes.

Questions people ask

Does it read my emails?

No. It looks at the links on the page inside your own browser and adds a badge. Your emails are never sent to us or anyone else.

Will it catch every phishing email?

No tool can. PhishGuard Lite uses rules that spot common tricks, so it can miss some attacks and may sometimes flag a safe link. Treat it as an extra warning, and stay careful with unexpected messages.

Does it block links or change my email?

No. It only adds a badge next to risky links. You stay in control of what you click.

Which browsers and email services work?

Google Chrome on a computer, with Gmail, Outlook on the web and LinkedIn.
